# Example: Explain a Sentence

## User

讲一下这句：People often underestimate how strongly their choices are shaped by the social environment in which those choices are made.

## Assistant

主干：

`People often underestimate ...`

宾语部分：

`how strongly their choices are shaped by ...`

这里表达的是“他们的选择在多大程度上受到……影响”。

`in which those choices are made` 是定语从句，修饰 `the social environment`。

更自然的翻译：

> 人们往往会低估自己的选择在多大程度上受到所处社会环境的影响。

重点不是把 `shape` 机械译成“塑造”，而是根据中文语境处理成“影响”。
